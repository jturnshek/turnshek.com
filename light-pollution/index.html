About This Project

This visualization explores the relationship between light pollution and socioeconomic factors in Pittsburgh neighborhoods using citizen science data from Globe at Night and US Census demographics.

How to Use

Map Layers: Toggle census tracts, observations, and neighborhoods.

Filters: Select years to view specific observation periods.

Click: Click any census tract to see demographics and light pollution data.

Data Sources

Globe at Night: Citizen science observations (2020-2024)
US Census Bureau: ACS 2022 5-Year Estimates
WPRDC: Pittsburgh geographic boundaries
City of Pittsburgh: LED streetlight tracker
